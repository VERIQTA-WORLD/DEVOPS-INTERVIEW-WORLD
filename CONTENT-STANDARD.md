# Interview content standard

## Questions

State the topic, intended level, assumptions and candidate task. Use accurate, sufficient evidence. Avoid revealing the answer in the question. Multiple-choice options should be plausible and similar in detail; do not make the correct option conspicuously longer.

## Answers

Explain the evidence, reasoning and alternatives. State uncertainty. Include follow-up questions, common misunderstandings and criteria for a strong response. Do not present one architecture or mitigation as universally correct.

## Practical assessments

Provide reproducible setup, a candidate brief, success criteria, optional hints, a separate solution, validation and cleanup. Verify the task and solution in the documented environment.

## Evaluation

Assess technical correctness, use of evidence, investigation sequence, trade-offs, security and communication. Distinguish essential criteria from optional depth.

## Integrity

Use original educational material and appropriately attributed public sources. Exclude confidential interview content, exam dumps, credentials and private incident data.
